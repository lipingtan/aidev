# 场景：王都城墙

## 视觉描述

> 用于序幕第3章莉娅娜与艾伦对话/巡逻段落

```
Top of a medieval city wall at night, wide stone battlements,
overlooking the dark landscape beyond the city, distant mountains barely visible,
iron torch brackets along the walkway, cold wind blowing snow,
a sense of being on the edge between civilization and wilderness
```

## 光线

| 光源 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 主光源 | iron torches along the wall, warm amber pools of light |
| 环境光 | cold blue moonlight, vast dark sky |
| 远景 | faint ominous purple glow on the distant horizon (black mist) |
