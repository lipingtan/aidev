# 空间视觉 DNA — 失落的王冠

> 本文件定义所有区域的视觉 DNA 词，供 scenes/*.md 和 shot_design 引用。
> 下级场景的提示词 = 上级 DNA 可见子集 + 本级特有描述。

---

## 世界级 DNA

> 整个艾伦迪尔世界共享的物理基调。所有人类建筑场景都应体现这些特征。

```
cold grey stone, weathered limestone, iron fittings,
medieval gothic architecture, pointed arches,
iron wall-mounted torch brackets, cobblestone ground
```

---

## 区域：王都艾伦迪尔

### 区域 DNA

```
cold grey stone walls, dark slate-grey rooftops,
gothic spires and towers, iron torch brackets on stone walls,
cobblestone streets worn smooth, frosted window panes,
snow-dusted surfaces, winter mist between buildings
```

### 标志性轮廓（远景可见）

```
clustered gothic spires against moonlit sky,
large central castle silhouette on elevated ground,
stone city walls with watchtowers,
warm amber torch lights scattered below like embers
```

### 材质与纹理（近景可见）

```
rough-hewn grey limestone blocks with mortar joints,
slight green moss in crevices, iron-hinged wooden doors,
frost crystals on stone surfaces, soot stains near torch brackets,
worn cobblestone with snow between gaps
```

### 光线特征

| 时段 | 光线描述 |
|------|----------|
| 夜晚（序幕主要时段） | cold blue moonlight from above, warm amber torch light on walls, deep shadows between buildings |
| 白天 | overcast grey sky, diffused cold light, no harsh shadows |
| 黄昏 | fading orange-grey light, long shadows, torches being lit |

## 区域：王宫（王都子区域）

### 区域 DNA

```
polished grey stone, larger blocks than city buildings,
ornate iron chandeliers, heavy oak doors with iron studs,
royal banners (faded gold on dark blue), vaulted ceilings,
cold grandeur, echoing emptiness
```

### 标志性轮廓（从城区看王宫）

```
imposing stone castle on elevated ground,
multiple towers with narrow windows,
heavy fortified gatehouse, royal banner hanging limp
```

### 材质与纹理（内部近景）

```
smooth polished stone floors, tapestries on walls (faded, dusty),
iron candelabras with half-melted candles,
carved stone pillars with worn heraldic symbols
```

## 区域：王宫密室（王宫子区域）

### 场所 DNA

```
ancient stone older than the palace above,
circular chamber with carved pillars,
forgotten symbols on walls, dark altar at center,
purple-black mist pooling on floor,
faint purple glow from floor cracks, oppressive low ceiling
```

### 材质与纹理

```
rough unpolished stone (older era than palace),
damp surfaces with condensation,
carved symbols filled with faint purple residue,
black iron torch brackets (cruder style than palace above)
```

## 区域：封印塔（王都边缘）

### 场所 DNA

```
tall solitary ancient tower, stone older than the city,
faintly glowing runes carved into surface,
visible cracks spreading across stone,
dark purple-black mist seeping from cracks,
dead vegetation in surrounding area, ominous isolation
```

### 状态变体

| 状态 | DNA 变化 |
|------|----------|
| 序幕（裂缝初现） | hairline cracks, faint purple glow, vegetation wilting |
| 第二幕（恶化） | wider cracks, mist actively seeping, vegetation dead and blackened |
| 第五幕（崩溃） | massive fractures, mist pouring out, runes flickering and dying |

## 区域：东方废墟

### 区域 DNA

```
ancient elven ruins overgrown with moss and vines,
white-grey stone with elegant curved architecture,
broken arches and collapsed domes,
bioluminescent moss in shadows, thin mist at ground level,
forest reclaiming the structures
```

### 标志性轮廓（远景可见）

```
broken white spires rising above dense forest canopy,
collapsed dome partially visible through mist,
ancient stone bridge spanning a ravine
```

### 材质与纹理（近景可见）

```
smooth white-grey stone with elven script carved in flowing lines,
thick green moss covering lower surfaces,
vine roots cracking through stone joints,
faint blue-white glow from intact rune carvings
```

### 光线特征

| 时段 | 光线描述 |
|------|----------|
| 白天 | diffused green-tinted light filtering through canopy, misty, dappled |
| 夜晚 | bioluminescent moss glow, faint blue rune light, deep forest darkness |

## 区域：北方冰原 / 北方要塞

### 区域 DNA

```
harsh frozen landscape, endless white snow plains,
brutal military architecture, thick stone walls reinforced with iron,
heavy fur and leather elements, military banners (frost-covered),
breath visible in extreme cold, ice formations on surfaces
```

### 标志性轮廓（远景可见）

```
massive squat fortress against white horizon,
thick walls with angular watchtowers,
smoke rising from chimneys, military camp outside walls
```

### 材质与纹理（近景可见）

```
rough dark stone with ice crystals in mortar,
thick iron reinforcement plates on doors and gates,
frozen condensation on metal surfaces,
heavy wooden beams blackened by smoke
```

### 光线特征

| 时段 | 光线描述 |
|------|----------|
| 白天 | harsh white overcast, blinding snow reflection, flat lighting |
| 夜晚 | deep blue darkness, orange firelight from braziers, aurora occasionally |
| 暴风雪 | near-zero visibility, white-out, only closest objects visible |

## 区域：南方据点

### 区域 DNA

```
warm-toned stone (sandstone and clay),
partially destroyed fortifications, makeshift repairs,
campfires and improvised shelters, refugee presence,
warmer climate but overcast skies, sense of last stand
```

### 标志性轮廓（远景可见）

```
low walls with gaps and rubble,
multiple campfire smoke columns rising,
patchwork of tents and stone buildings,
farmland visible beyond (abandoned, overgrown)
```

### 材质与纹理（近景可见）

```
warm sandstone with battle damage (sword marks, scorch),
wooden barricades nailed together hastily,
mud-stained canvas tents, scattered supplies,
iron weapons stacked against walls
```

## 区域：影龙山脉

### 区域 DNA

```
black volcanic rock and bone-white formations,
perpetual dark purple-black mist obscuring sky,
no living vegetation, petrified trees,
purple lightning in clouds above,
ground cracked with glowing purple fissures,
oppressive supernatural darkness
```

### 标志性轮廓（远景可见）

```
jagged black mountain peaks piercing purple-black clouds,
massive bone-like rock formations,
rivers of dark mist flowing downhill,
faint purple glow from mountain core
```

### 材质与纹理（近景可见）

```
sharp black obsidian-like rock surfaces,
bone-white calcium deposits in organic shapes,
purple crystalline growths in crevices,
ground warm to touch with faint vibration
```

## 区域：重建王都（尾声）

### 区域 DNA

```
same grey stone as original city but cleaned and repaired,
new green growth (vines, saplings) on buildings,
morning golden sunlight, clear blue sky,
scaffolding on some buildings (reconstruction in progress),
iron torch brackets now holding flower baskets,
the Ironblood Tree at city center (new landmark)
```

### 与原王都的视觉对比

| 元素 | 序幕王都 | 尾声王都 |
|------|----------|----------|
| 石材 | 冷灰+青苔+霜 | 同样灰石但清洁，有新绿 |
| 天空 | 夜晚/阴沉 | 朝阳/晴朗 |
| 植被 | 无/枯萎 | 新芽/藤蔓/花 |
| 氛围 | 压抑孤寂 | 希望新生 |
| 标志物 | 封印塔（阴暗） | 铁血之树（生机） |
