# 场景：重建后的王都（尾声）

## 空间关系

- 上级：与原王都同一位置，但战后重建
- 时间：决战后1~3年

## 重建王都全景

```
The royal city of Arendil being rebuilt under warm golden sunlight,
scaffolding on repaired stone walls, new green vines growing on old stone,
citizens working together, smoke from chimneys, sounds of construction,
a sense of hope and renewal, the sky is clear blue for the first time
```

## 铁血之树（中央广场）

```
A magnificent tree in the center of a rebuilt city square,
trunk intertwined with golden dragon scales, silver starlight leaves,
red flame-like flowers, and green living vines,
a golden crown embedded in the trunk, glowing softly,
people gathering around it, warm sunlight filtering through its canopy
```

## 影龙山脉（尾声版/新绿）

```
The former dragon mountains now covered in new green vegetation,
the dark stone softened by moss and young trees growing from cracks,
sunlight reaching the peaks for the first time in years,
a sense of nature reclaiming what was lost, peaceful and hopeful
```

## 光线

| 视角 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 全景 | warm golden sunlight, clear blue sky, first time without mist |
| 铁血之树 | dappled sunlight through magical canopy, soft golden glow from crown |
| 山脉新绿 | natural sunlight, green and gold tones, peaceful |

## 环境锚点

- 石材：same cold grey stone but now with green vines and repairs
- 特殊：warm sunlight（对比之前所有场景的夜晚/阴天）, new growth, hope
- 天气：clear sky, warm, first sunlight
