# 场景：王都酒馆

## 外部

> 用于艾伦走出酒馆的镜头

```
Exterior of a rough medieval tavern at night, heavy wooden door with iron hinges,
warm amber light spilling from inside when door opens,
a hanging wooden sign creaking in the wind, snow on the doorstep,
sounds of rowdy laughter from within, cobblestone street outside
```

## 内部

> 如需酒馆内部镜头

```
A dimly lit medieval tavern interior, rough wooden tables and benches,
flickering candlelight and oil lamps, stone walls with weapon marks,
spilled ale on the floor, rowdy atmosphere, smoke haze in the air
```

| 视角 | 光源 | 英文关键词 |
|------|------|-----------|

| 内部 | 蜡烛+油灯 | flickering candlelight, dim oil lamps, smoke-diffused amber glow |
