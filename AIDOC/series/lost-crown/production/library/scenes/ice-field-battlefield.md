# 场景：冰原战场（独立战斗场景）

> 第二幕~第三幕大规模战役的主战场，区别于北方要塞内部。

## 开阔冰原战场

```
A vast open frozen battlefield stretching in all directions,
armies clashing in blizzard conditions, snow stained with blood,
black mist creatures surging forward in waves against human formations,
fire magic explosions creating orange bursts against white landscape,
fallen soldiers and broken weapons scattered across the ice,
military banners torn and flapping in howling wind
```

## 战场——联军冲锋

```
Aerial view of a military charge across frozen ground,
thousands of soldiers in formation advancing through snow,
cavalry on the flanks, infantry with shields raised,
banners of blue-silver flying, breath visible in the cold,
determination on frost-bitten faces
```

## 战场——惨败撤退

```
Overhead shot of a chaotic battlefield in disarray,
black mist creatures overwhelming scattered human formations,
soldiers fleeing in disorder, wounded being dragged,
fires and magic explosions dotting the white landscape,
black mist advancing like a tidal wave consuming everything
```

## 战场——魔法对抗

```
A focal point on the battlefield where magic collides:
orange-red fire magic pushing back purple-black mist,
silver-white starlight forming a dome shield over retreating soldiers,
green vine magic erupting from the ground to entangle shadow creatures,
gold-black dragon energy slashing through enemy lines,
dramatic color contrast against the white snow backdrop
```

## 光线

| 状态 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 战前 | pale grey overcast sky, ominous calm, snow falling lightly |
| 激战 | fire magic orange vs black mist purple vs white snow, chaotic lighting |
| 惨败 | darkening sky, black mist blocking light, only fire providing illumination |
| 战后 | grey silence, snow beginning to cover the dead, distant fires dying |

---

## 环境锚点

- 地形：完全开阔平坦冰原，无遮蔽
- 天气：暴风雪为常态，能见度低
- 特殊：黑雾从北方（影龙山脉方向）涌来
- 规模感：必须体现"千军万马"——至少数千人的军队规模
