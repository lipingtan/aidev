# 场景：影龙山脉

## 空间关系

- 上级：世界最北端，黑雾的源头
- 地形：黑色骨骼状山脉，地面寸草不生，紫色裂缝遍布

## 山脉远景

```
A massive dark mountain range against a sky completely covered in swirling black mist,
jagged peaks resembling dragon bones and spines,
purple-black lightning crackling between the peaks,
no vegetation, ground is cracked dark stone with glowing purple fissures,
an overwhelming sense of ancient evil and dread
```

## 山脉入口/战场

```
The base of the dragon mountains, a vast barren plain of cracked dark stone,
armies gathered for final assault, military formations visible,
black mist pouring down from the mountains like a waterfall,
purple lightning illuminating the battlefield, epic scale
```

## 影龙王巢穴（山顶）

```
The peak of the highest mountain, a massive crater-like arena of black bone and stone,
the sky above is a vortex of swirling black mist,
ancient dragon bones embedded in the ground, glowing purple cracks everywhere,
an altar-like formation at the center where the Shadow Dragon King manifests
```

## 光线

| 视角 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 远景 | no natural light, purple-black mist glow, lightning flashes |
| 战场 | fire magic and star magic providing light against total darkness |
| 巢穴 | pulsing purple glow from cracks, black mist vortex above, no sky visible |

## 环境锚点

- 石材：black volcanic stone with purple glowing cracks（完全不同于王都）
- 特殊：dragon bone formations, no vegetation, black mist everywhere
- 天气：no sky visible, perpetual darkness, purple lightning
