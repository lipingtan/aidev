# 场景：封印塔

## 视觉描述

```
A tall ancient stone tower at the edge of the royal city,
covered in faintly glowing ancient runes, visible cracks spreading across its surface,
dark purple-black mist seeping from the cracks,
surrounded by dead vegetation, an ominous aura radiating outward,
interior spiral staircase with crumbling steps, a central sealed chamber at the top
```

## 光线

| 光源 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 外部 | cold moonlight on grey stone, faint purple glow from cracks |
| 内部 | ancient runes glowing faintly blue-white, dark purple mist providing eerie illumination |
| 氛围 | ominous, ancient, power barely contained |
