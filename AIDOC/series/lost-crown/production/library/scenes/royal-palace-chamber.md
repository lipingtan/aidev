# 场景：王宫密室

> 序幕中国王与维克多密谋的场所，黑雾渗透的核心。

## 密室全景

```
A hidden underground chamber beneath the royal palace,
circular stone room with ancient pillars carved with forgotten symbols,
a single dark altar at the center with purple-black mist pooling around it,
dim torches on walls casting long shadows, cold damp air,
the feeling of something ancient and forbidden,
faint purple glow from cracks in the floor
```

## 密室——密谋进行时

```
Two figures in the underground chamber: a tired king on a stone seat
and a tall aristocrat standing over him, conspiratorial atmosphere,
the altar's purple mist reaching toward them like curious tendrils,
torch light flickering as if disturbed by unseen presence,
shadows on the walls seeming to move independently
```

## 密室——扎克里仪式

```
A gaunt robed figure before the dark altar, arms raised,
chanting with inhuman cadence, purple ritual symbols glowing on his hands,
black mist rising from the altar in thick columns,
the chamber walls seeming to pulse with dark energy,
purple lightning crackling between the pillars
```

## 光线

| 状态 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 空置 | dim torches, purple glow from floor cracks, oppressive darkness |
| 密谋 | torch light on faces, deep shadows, conspiratorial intimacy |
| 仪式 | purple ritual glow dominant, torches dimming, supernatural light |

---

## 环境锚点

- 位于王宫地下深处，需要通过隐藏通道进入
- 石材比王宫更古老（建城之前就存在的遗迹）
- 空气中有微弱的腐蚀感（黑雾长期渗透）
- 祭坛上的符文与东方废墟的精灵符文相似但扭曲
