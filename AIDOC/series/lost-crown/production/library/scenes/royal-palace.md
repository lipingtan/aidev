# 场景：王宫

## 外景（夜）

> 用于过渡镜头

```
A grand medieval castle at night, tall dark stone walls and iron gates,
ominous silhouette against a cold moonlit sky, crows perched on battlements,
faint torchlight from narrow windows, an oppressive regal atmosphere
```

## 召见大厅

```
A grand medieval throne hall, tall stone pillars with iron torch brackets,
cold grey stone floor, a raised throne at the far end,
banners hanging from high ceiling, dim torchlight casting long shadows,
oppressive atmosphere despite the grandeur
```

## 密室

```
A small dark chamber deep within the palace, stone walls with no windows,
a single black candle on a stone table, faint purple-black mist seeping from cracks,
ancient symbols carved into the walls, suffocating and secretive atmosphere
```

## 走廊

> 用于维克多离开密室的镜头

```
A long dark palace corridor, stone walls with iron torch brackets,
flickering amber torchlight casting elongated shadows,
cold stone floor, oppressive silence
```
