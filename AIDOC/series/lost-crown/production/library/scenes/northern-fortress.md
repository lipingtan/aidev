# 场景：北方冰原与要塞

## 空间关系

- 上级：王都北方，约5天行军距离
- 地形：广阔冰原平原，远处可见影龙山脉轮廓

## 冰原全景

```
A vast frozen tundra stretching to the horizon, endless white snow under a pale grey sky,
distant dark mountain range barely visible through blizzard haze,
howling wind carrying snow horizontally, desolate and unforgiving landscape,
occasional dark shapes of dead trees frozen in ice
```

## 北方要塞（外部）

```
A massive stone fortress on the frozen plain, thick walls covered in frost and ice,
military banners (blue and silver) snapping in the wind,
heavy iron gates, watchtowers with burning braziers,
soldiers in fur-lined armor patrolling the battlements,
built to withstand both cold and siege
```

## 北方要塞（内部/大厅）

```
A large military command hall inside the fortress, rough stone walls with war maps,
a central table with tactical markers, iron chandeliers with candles,
fur pelts on benches, breath visible in the cold air despite the fireplace,
functional and austere, no luxury
```

## 冰原战场

```
Open frozen battlefield, armies clashing in blizzard conditions,
black mist creatures emerging from the snow, fire magic lighting up the white landscape,
blood on snow, fallen soldiers, chaos of medieval combat in extreme cold
```

## 环境锚点

- 石材：same cold grey stone as royal city（同一王国建筑风格）
- 特殊：ice and frost on everything, military banners blue and silver, fur-lined armor
- 天气：blizzard, howling wind, extreme cold
