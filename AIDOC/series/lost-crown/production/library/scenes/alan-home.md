# 场景：艾伦与艾薇儿的居所

## 空间关系

- 上级：王都街巷（安静小巷尽头）
- 外部可见：从街巷看到一扇透出暖光的窗户
- 内部窗户：可看到外面的雪夜街巷

## 外部

> 从街巷视角看居所

```
A small modest stone dwelling at the end of a quiet alley,
cold grey stone walls with slight moss (same style as city buildings),
a single frosted window glowing with warm orange firelight from within,
snow on the doorstep and windowsill, iron-hinged wooden door,
iron wall-mounted torch bracket beside the door (unlit)
```

## 内部

```
A small modest stone dwelling interior, warm fireplace glow,
simple wooden furniture, a single window with frost,
cozy but sparse interior, a worn blanket on a wooden bench,
silver starlight filtering through the frosted window,
cold grey stone walls matching the exterior
```

## 光线

| 视角 | 光源 | 英文关键词 |
|------|------|-----------|

| 内部主光 | 壁炉 | warm orange fireplace glow, dancing shadows |

本场景继承全局锚点：cold grey stone, medieval gothic, snowy night, iron fittings
