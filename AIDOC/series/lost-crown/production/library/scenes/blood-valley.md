# 场景：血谷前哨

## 空间关系

- 上级：王都西南方向，约2天行军距离
- 地形：狭窄峡谷，红色岩壁，谷底有古老战场遗迹

## 全景（远景）

```
A narrow canyon with towering red-brown rock walls on both sides,
dark purple-black mist pooling at the canyon floor,
ancient battle scars on the rock faces, rusted weapons embedded in stone,
overcast sky barely visible between the cliff tops, oppressive and claustrophobic
```

## 前哨营地

```
A makeshift military camp at the mouth of a red rock canyon,
wooden palisades and canvas tents, iron torch brackets,
soldiers in worn armor standing guard, campfires casting orange light,
the canyon entrance behind them shrouded in dark mist
```

## 光线

| 视角 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 全景 | overcast diffused light, dark purple mist glow from below, red rock reflecting dim light |
| 营地 | campfire orange glow, iron torches, cold grey sky above |
| 战斗中 | fire magic orange-red vs black mist purple, dramatic contrast |

## 环境锚点

- 石材：red-brown canyon rock（区别于王都的 cold grey stone）
- 特殊：black mist pooling at ground level, ancient rusted weapons in rock
