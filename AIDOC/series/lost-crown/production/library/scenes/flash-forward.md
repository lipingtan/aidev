# 场景：闪前开场（Flash Forward Opening）

> 全片第一个画面，3秒闪前钩子，在序幕之前播放。

## 画面1（1秒）：影龙王展翼

```
A colossal dragon silhouette against a sky filled with purple lightning,
bone wings spreading impossibly wide blocking all light,
black mist exploding outward from the dragon's form,
extreme wide shot, overwhelming scale, pure terror
```

## 画面2（1秒）：四色光柱

```
Four intertwining pillars of light shooting into the dark sky:
gold-black, silver-white, orange-red, emerald-green,
spiraling together piercing through black mist,
four small human silhouettes at the base of the light pillar,
epic scale, hope against darkness
```

## 画面3（1秒）：艾伦半怪物化

```
Extreme close-up of a young man's face, half-transformed:
left side normal with grey eye, right side covered in gold-black dragon scales,
right eye glowing molten gold, mouth open in an anguished scream,
veins of gold-black light spreading across his neck,
rain and embers falling around him, pure agony and determination
```

## 转场

```
Hard cut to black screen.
White text fading in: "3 years earlier"
Fade to: aerial view of a medieval city at night, snow falling gently
```

---

## 技术说明

- 三个画面之间硬切，无过渡
- 每个画面1秒，总计3秒
- 无对话，仅环境音效（龙吼、光柱嗡鸣、嘶吼）
- 色调：极暗紫黑 → 四色爆发 → 特写暖冷对比
- 目的：制造悬念钩子，让观众想知道"发生了什么"
