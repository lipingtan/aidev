# 场景：东方废墟

## 视觉描述

```
Ancient elven ruins overgrown with moss and vines,
crumbling stone pillars and archways, soft green bioluminescent plants,
misty atmosphere, dappled light through broken canopy,
ancient carved symbols on stone walls
```
