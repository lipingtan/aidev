# 场景：南方据点

## 空间关系

- 上级：王都南方，联军最后退守的要塞
- 地形：丘陵地带，气候比北方温和，但城墙残破

## 据点全景

```
A battered stone fortress on a hilltop, walls cracked and partially collapsed,
makeshift repairs with wooden beams and canvas, campfires scattered around,
overcast sky with occasional breaks of warm sunlight,
a sense of last stand, desperate but not yet defeated
```

## 据点内部

```
Interior of a damaged fortress hall, cracked stone walls patched with wood,
a large table covered in maps and reports, candles and oil lamps,
wounded soldiers resting on makeshift beds along the walls,
a central fireplace providing warmth, exhausted but determined atmosphere
```

## 塔楼（艾伦远眺）

```
Top of a damaged watchtower, open to the sky, crumbling battlements,
overlooking the landscape below, distant black mist visible on the northern horizon,
wind blowing through broken stonework, a solitary figure standing at the edge
```

| 视角 | 英文关键词 |

## 环境锚点

- 石材：same cold grey stone but cracked and damaged
- 特殊：makeshift repairs, wounded soldiers, maps everywhere, last stand atmosphere
- 天气：overcast, warmer than north, occasional sunlight
