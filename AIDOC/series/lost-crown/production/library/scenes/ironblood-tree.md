# 场景：铁血之树

> 尾声核心场景，象征新生与守护。位于重建王都中央广场。

## 铁血之树（新生期）

```
A magnificent tree in the center of a rebuilt city square,
trunk intertwined with four colors: gold-black bark (dragon blood),
silver-white luminescent leaves (starlight), orange-red ember-like flowers (flame),
emerald-green living vines wrapping the trunk (nature),
a golden crown embedded in the trunk's center, glowing softly,
new green shoots sprouting from the base, warm golden sunlight filtering through leaves
```

## 铁血之树（成熟期/尾声后期）

```
A towering ancient-looking tree despite its youth, four-colored canopy spreading wide,
the embedded crown now partially grown over by bark,
children playing beneath its branches, dappled four-colored light on the ground,
a sense of peace and protection radiating from the tree
```

## 铁血之树（彩蛋1——裂纹）

```
Close-up of the tree trunk, deep within the bark a thin black crack barely visible,
faint purple glow pulsing within the crack like a heartbeat,
otherwise the tree appears healthy and vibrant, the crack almost imperceptible
```

## 铁血之树（彩蛋2——银发女孩）

```
A young girl about 8 years old with silver hair touching the tree trunk,
her left eye suddenly glowing with a silver star-shaped mark,
looking up with wonder and slight fear, golden afternoon light,
the tree's leaves rustling without wind above her
```

## 光线

| 时段 | 英文关键词 |
|------|-----------|
| 种植时（夕阳） | warm golden sunset light, long shadows, hopeful atmosphere |
| 日常（白天） | dappled four-colored light through leaves, warm and peaceful |
| 誓言时（月光） | soft moonlight, tree glowing faintly with all four colors |
| 彩蛋（午后） | golden afternoon light, peaceful, slightly ominous undertone |

---

## 环境锚点

- 位于重建王都中央广场
- 周围是新建的石头建筑（比旧王都更简朴但更温暖）
- 广场地面铺设新石板，树周围有环形石凳
- 四色光芒在夜间可从远处看到（城市地标）
