# 场景：通用营地

> 贯穿全片的营地场景，根据所在区域调整环境色调。

## 营地全景

```
A military encampment at night, rows of canvas tents arranged in orderly lines,
multiple campfires sending sparks into the dark sky,
soldiers in fur-lined cloaks huddled around fires,
horses tethered nearby, supply wagons at the perimeter,
a command tent larger than the rest with a banner flying above
```

## 篝火特写（情感场景核心）

```
A single campfire with orange flames crackling, embers floating upward,
warm light illuminating faces in a circle, darkness beyond the firelight,
bedrolls and weapons nearby, intimate atmosphere within the vast dark
```

## 指挥帐篷内部

```
Interior of a large military tent, a wooden table with tactical maps,
candles and oil lamps providing warm light, fur rugs on the ground,
weapon racks along canvas walls, a sense of temporary but organized living
```
