# 小乞儿托姆 — 视觉关键词（提示词专用）

## 素体描述（按年龄分版本）

### 10岁版本（序幕~第一幕）

```
A scrawny street urchin, about 10 years old, dirty face,
bright clever brown eyes, messy brown hair,
ragged oversized clothes, barefoot, small and quick
```

### 12岁版本（第三幕~第五幕）

```
A lean boy of about 12, slightly taller, same clever brown eyes,
wearing an ill-fitting military uniform too large for him,
determined expression replacing childhood innocence, still small but wiry
```

### 13岁版本（尾声）

```
A lean teenage boy in a simple training tunic, brown hair neatly cut,
determined expression, practicing sword forms under a glowing tree,
clumsy but earnest movements, growing into his frame
```
