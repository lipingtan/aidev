# 敌方单位 — 视觉关键词（提示词专用）

> 非命名敌方单位，按威胁等级排列。战斗场景中根据剧情阶段选用。

---

## 小兵级：影雾兽 (Shadow Mist Beast)

```
A semi-transparent black mist quadruped, wolf-sized (shoulder height 1 meter),
red glowing eye slits, smoke-like edges dissolving into air,
dark claws leaving black marks on ground, moving in packs,
body partially see-through with darker core
```

| 属性 | 值 |
|------|-----|
| 体型 | 大型犬（肩高约1米） |
| 弱点 | 火焰/星辰光直接驱散 |
| 出现频率 | 大量，最常见 |
| 首次出现 | 序幕（1~2只试探） |

---

## 精英级：影骨骑士 (Shadow Bone Knight)

```
A humanoid skeleton wrapped in dense black mist, 2 meters tall,
corroded ancient armor fragments clinging to bones,
purple flame burning in empty eye sockets,
wielding a black longsword, dark mist shockwave trailing each swing,
deliberate menacing movements, ancient warrior's stance
```

| 属性 | 值 |
|------|-----|
| 体型 | 2米人形 |
| 弱点 | 胸腔紫色核心击碎即死；藤蔓可束缚 |
| 出现频率 | 少量（3~5只配合影雾兽群） |
| 首次出现 | 第一幕 |

---

## 大型级：影蛛母 (Shadow Broodmother)

```
A massive spider-like creature, 4 meters wide,
eight bone-spike legs emerging from black mist body,
dark red compound eyes clustered on head,
crown-like bone spikes arranged on its back,
abdomen pulsing as it spawns smaller mist beasts,
black mist webbing dripping from mandibles
```

| 属性 | 值 |
|------|-----|
| 体型 | 体宽约4米 |
| 弱点 | 腹部核心（火焰焚烧后暴露）；切断骨刺腿削弱移动 |
| 出现频率 | 关键战斗1~2只 |
| 首次出现 | 第二幕 |

---

## 大型级：影龙幼体 (Shadow Dragonling)

```
A small dragon form, 6-meter wingspan,
bone wings covered with black mist membrane,
half-skeleton half-mist body, ribs visible through dark fog,
bone-spiked tail whipping behind,
breathing a cone of black mist instead of fire,
smaller and more agile than the dragon king
```

| 属性 | 值 |
|------|-----|
| 体型 | 翼展约6米 |
| 弱点 | 翼膜（火焰焚烧使其坠落）；星辰魔法压制吐息 |
| 出现频率 | 每次1~2只 |
| 首次出现 | 第三幕 |

---

## 战斗场景出现规律

| 剧情阶段 | 敌方组合 | 战斗规模 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 序幕 | 1~2只影雾兽 | 极小 |
| 第一幕 | 影雾兽群(10~20) + 1~2影骨骑士 | 小规模 |
| 第二幕 | 影雾兽大群 + 影骨骑士小队 + 首次影蛛母 | 中规模 |
| 第三幕 | 影骨骑士为主 + 影龙幼体首次 | 中大规模 |
| 第四幕 | 全种类混合 + 多只影龙幼体 | 大规模 |
| 第五幕 | 全种类 + 影龙王最终形态 | 史诗级 |

---

## 通用战斗氛围关键词

```
black mist creatures emerging from darkness,
red and purple glowing eyes in the fog,
the sound of bones scraping, unnatural movement,
mist dissipating when struck by fire or starlight
```
