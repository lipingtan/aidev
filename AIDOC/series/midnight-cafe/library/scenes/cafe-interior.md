# 场景：月光咖啡馆内部（夜）

## 视觉关键词
```
A small vintage café at night, warm amber pendant lamps, dark wooden bar counter, 
wall of black and white photographs, small round tables, worn leather chairs, 
vintage brass espresso machine, rain-streaked windows
```

## 光线
- 主光源：warm pendant lamp, amber glow from above
- 辅助光：soft candlelight on tables
- 环境光：cool blue streetlight through rain-streaked windows
