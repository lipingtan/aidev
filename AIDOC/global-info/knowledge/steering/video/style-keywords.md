# 风格关键词库

> 跨引擎通用的风格关键词，按类别组织。

## 画面风格

| 风格 | 关键词 | 适用场景 |
|------|--------|----------|
| 电影感 | cinematic, 35mm film, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field | 叙事短剧、微电影 |
| 纪录片 | documentary style, handheld, natural light, raw footage | 真实感、纪实 |
| 复古 | vintage, film grain, faded colors, retro color grading | 回忆、年代感 |
| 赛博朋克 | neon noir, cyberpunk, holographic, rain-soaked streets | 科幻、未来 |
| 温暖治愈 | warm tones, soft focus, golden hour, cozy atmosphere | 治愈系、日常 |
| 冷峻悬疑 | desaturated, cold blue tones, harsh shadows, noir lighting | 悬疑、犯罪 |
| 动漫风 | anime style, cel shading, vibrant colors, dynamic angles | 二次元 |
| 奇幻史诗 | epic fantasy, volumetric light, grand scale, mythical | 奇幻、魔幻 |
| 极简 | minimalist, clean lines, negative space, muted palette | 文艺、高级感 |

## 光线类型

| 类型 | 关键词 | 情绪效果 |
|------|--------|----------|
| 自然光 | natural sunlight, overcast diffused light, golden hour | 真实、温暖 |
| 人工光 | neon lights, fluorescent, warm lamplight, candlelight | 氛围感 |
| 戏剧光 | volumetric light, rim lighting, chiaroscuro, Tyndall effect | 戏剧性、神秘 |
| 环境光 | ambient glow, reflected light, bounced light | 柔和、自然 |
| 逆光 | backlit, silhouette, rim light, halo effect | 神秘、美感 |
| 顶光 | overhead light, harsh downlight | 审讯感、压迫 |
| 底光 | underlight, upward shadows | 恐怖、诡异 |

## 情绪氛围

| 情绪 | 关键词 | 配合光线 |
|------|--------|----------|
| 温馨 | warm, intimate, cozy, gentle, tender | 暖色柔光 |
| 紧张 | tense, suspenseful, uneasy, claustrophobic | 硬光、暗调 |
| 忧伤 | melancholic, somber, wistful, bittersweet | 冷调柔光 |
| 欢快 | joyful, vibrant, energetic, playful | 明亮自然光 |
| 神秘 | mysterious, ethereal, dreamlike, surreal | 体积光、雾气 |
| 史诗 | epic, grand, majestic, awe-inspiring | 戏剧光、大范围 |
| 恐怖 | eerie, ominous, dread, unsettling | 底光、闪烁 |
| 浪漫 | romantic, dreamy, soft, enchanting | 逆光、柔焦 |

## 色调/调色

| 色调 | 关键词 | 情绪 |
|------|--------|------|
| 暖调 | warm color grading, amber tones, golden palette | 温暖、怀旧 |
| 冷调 | cool tones, blue-grey palette, desaturated | 冷峻、疏离 |
| 高对比 | high contrast, deep blacks, bright highlights | 戏剧性 |
| 低对比 | low contrast, flat lighting, muted | 平静、忧郁 |
| 单色 | monochromatic, sepia, black and white | 艺术感、回忆 |
| 霓虹 | neon colors, vibrant saturated, electric | 赛博、夜生活 |
| 柔和 | pastel, soft palette, muted colors | 温柔、梦幻 |

## 质感/纹理

| 质感 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 胶片感 | film grain, 35mm texture, analog feel |
| 数字清晰 | sharp, crisp, high definition, clean |
| 梦幻 | soft focus, lens flare, bokeh, diffusion |
| 粗粝 | gritty, raw, textured, rough |
| 丝滑 | smooth, polished, refined, sleek |
