# KLING 情绪表达模式

> 验证有效的情绪表达提示词模式。

---

## 模式：悲伤/忧郁

**适用场景**：角色处于悲伤、失落状态
**验证次数**：待积累

### 有效写法
```
Close-up, slow push in: [角色]'s eyes are downcast, a slight furrow between 
the brows. Lips pressed thin, jaw tight. A single tear forms at the corner 
of the eye but doesn't fall.
(Duration: 4 seconds)
```

### 无效写法
```
❌ She looks very sad and heartbroken.
（抽象情绪词，模型无法视觉化）
```

### 为什么有效
- 用具体的面部肌肉描述替代抽象情绪词
- 眼神方向 + 眉头 + 嘴唇 = 完整的情绪表达
- "forms but doesn't fall" 比 "crying" 更有张力

### 标签
#emotion #sadness #facial-expression #close-up

---

## 模式：愤怒/压抑

**适用场景**：角色愤怒但试图压制
**验证次数**：待积累

### 有效写法
```
Medium close-up: [角色]'s hand clenches into a fist at his side, 
knuckles whitening. His breathing deepens, nostrils flaring slightly. 
Eyes fixed forward, unblinking.
(Duration: 3 seconds)
```

### 关键要点
- 愤怒通过身体紧张表现（握拳、呼吸加深、鼻翼扩张）
- 压抑的愤怒比爆发更有视觉张力
- 配合紧凑的镜头时长

### 标签
#emotion #anger #restraint #body-language

---

## 模式：温暖/幸福

**适用场景**：温馨、幸福的情绪时刻
**验证次数**：待积累

### 有效写法
```
Medium shot, warm lamplight: [角色] smiles softly, the corners of her eyes 
crinkling. She tilts her head slightly, a gentle exhale visible in the 
cool evening air.
(Duration: 4 seconds)
```

### 关键要点
- 温暖情绪配合暖色光线
- 微笑要具体（眼角皱纹、嘴角弧度）
- 加入呼吸等生理细节增加真实感

### 标签
#emotion #warmth #happiness #soft-lighting

---

## 模式：恐惧/紧张

**适用场景**：角色感到恐惧或极度紧张
**验证次数**：待积累

### 有效写法
```
Close-up, slight handheld shake: [角色]'s pupils dilate, breath catching 
in her throat. She swallows hard, eyes darting to the left. 
Beads of sweat forming at the temple.
(Duration: 3 seconds)
```

### 关键要点
- 手持晃动增强不安感
- 瞳孔、呼吸、汗水 = 恐惧的生理反应
- 眼神快速移动暗示威胁来源
- 短时长增加紧迫感

### 标签
#emotion #fear #tension #handheld

---

## 情绪表达通用规则

| 情绪 | 面部关键词 | 身体关键词 | 推荐光线 |
|------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| 悲伤 | downcast eyes, furrowed brow, pressed lips | slumped shoulders, slow movement | 冷调柔光 |
| 愤怒 | narrowed eyes, clenched jaw, flared nostrils | clenched fists, rigid posture | 硬光、高对比 |
| 温暖 | crinkling eyes, soft smile, relaxed brow | open posture, gentle gestures | 暖色柔光 |
| 恐惧 | wide eyes, dilated pupils, parted lips | trembling, backing away, swallowing | 暗调、侧光 |
| 惊讶 | raised eyebrows, open mouth, widened eyes | sudden stillness, sharp inhale | 明亮、突然 |
| 决心 | steady gaze, set jaw, slight nod | squared shoulders, firm stance | 正面光、清晰 |
