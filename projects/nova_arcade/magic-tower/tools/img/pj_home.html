Pixel Art Challenge: Hydra and Seek

Pixel Loop 25

Draw a game card using the provided template.

Oh noo you just woke up from a nightmare, and now all the animals in the world have 2 or more head. Seek and catch ‘em all!!
Look up the threatened species levels of animal you want to draw, the threatened level
EX - EXtinct
Stats Max 10
EW - Extinct in the Wild
Stats Max 9
RE - Regionally Extinct
Stats Max 8
CR - Critically Endangered 
Stats Max 7
EN - Endangerd
Stats Max 6
VU - Vulnerable
Stats Max 5
LR - Lower Risk
Stats Max 4
LC - Least Concern
Stats Max 3 
DD - Data Deficient
Stats Max ???

the number of Health (Hearts) are equal to your animal Heads, the 2 heads = 2 hearts
 
you can choose to use the hearts or asset provided or draw it yourself just keep the tamplate of the border and the placement of the asset the same place.

Template: https://i.lospec.com/9avl.png

Example: https://i.lospec.com/haib.png

Canvas - 97 x 140 px Use tamplate.
Colors - Max 33 (use the https://lospec.com/palette-list/carnival-32)
Transparancy - Required (corners of the template).
Animation - Forbidden.



The challenge thread will have all the challenge updates. The thread is also a great place to post your WIPs.
